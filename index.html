Master’s Thesis: Open Earth Channel Drainage Design & Climate Analysis
Çatalkaya Watershed • 7 Provinces Across Türkiye
Thesis Research

Designed trapezoidal open earth drainage channels for seven provinces representing different climatic regions of Türkiye to assess regional climate variability on channel dimensions and flow capacity.

Utilized Turkish State Meteorological Service rainfall IDF data, McMath runoff estimation, and Manning hydraulic equations. Proposed retention ponds and vegetated swales as nature-based solutions.
