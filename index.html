Master’s Thesis: Open Earth Channel Drainage Design & Climate Analysis

Çatalkaya Watershed | 7 Provinces (Bursa, Aydın, Mersin, Kırşehir, Ordu, Muş, Şanlıurfa)

Designed trapezoidal open earth drainage channels for seven provinces representing different climatic regions of Türkiye to assess regional climate variability on channel width, depth, slope, and flow capacity.

Utilized Turkish State Meteorological Service rainfall intensity–duration (IDF) data and applied the McMath empirical method for runoff estimation (>25 km² watershed). Performed hydraulic sizing using the Manning equation.

Proposed nature-based solutions (retention pond & vegetated swale channel) to increase temporary storage, promote infiltration, and enhance flood resilience.
