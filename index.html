Regional Climate Variability & Hydraulic Drainage Design (Türkiye)

Designed trapezoidal open earth drainage channels across seven provinces representing distinct climatic regions of Türkiye: Bursa, Aydın, Mersin, Kırşehir, Ordu, Muş, and Şanlıurfa. Evaluated hydraulic performance and channel dimensions to assess how regional climate variability influences parameters such as channel width, depth, longitudinal slope, and flow capacity.

  • Hydrologic Modeling: Obtained intensity–duration rainfall data from the Turkish State Meteorological Service to construct IDF curves for each province. Applied the McMath empirical method (used in Türkiye for watersheds > 25 km²) to calculate runoff for the Çatalkaya Watershed.
  • Hydraulic Sizing: Utilized the Manning equation to size trapezoidal open earth channels designed to safely convey peak flood discharge.
  • Nature-Based Solutions (NBS): Integrated retention ponds and vegetated swale channels to improve flood resilience, increase temporary storage, promote infiltration, and reduce peak runoff entering the system.
